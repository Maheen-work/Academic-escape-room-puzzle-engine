Why protect them?

Safe nurseries

Mangroves shelter baby fish and crabs that grow up to feed our coast.

Storm shields

Mangrove roots slow big waves and protect the land from floods.

Balanced nature

Dolphins, cats and otters keep the number of fish just right.

Clean air

Mangrove mud stores carbon and helps cool our planet.

About this project

This website helps children aged 5 to 13 in Karachi learn about the wildlife of their own coast, with pictures, simple facts and animal sounds, all in one place.

Team

  • Zunairah Ahmed (group leader): page layout, navbar, animal data, gallery and detail window
  • Maheen Shafiq: all CSS, design, responsive layout and accessibility
  • Shanzay Siddiqui: sound button, search and filter, quiz, form and credits

Credits

  • Photos: the team’s chosen animal photos (original sources to be credited here).
  • Sounds: simple placeholder sounds. Turtles show “no sound available”. Real recordings from xeno-canto or Wikimedia will be credited here.
  • Population numbers are estimates chosen by the team, not official counts.
  • Sighting numbers: live open data from GBIF (gbif.org), updated automatically while the site is open.
  • The reference design was created with help from Claude (AI assistant); final code is written and understood by the team.
